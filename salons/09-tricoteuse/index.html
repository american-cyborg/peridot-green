PGI. Symbolic Symbiotic

Our first salon is an exploration of the symbols of American Cyborg. It is named for the generative relationship of a shared network of meaning and meaning-makers.

Cyborg Mandala, Bryan McGovern Wilson, 2019

This sculptural object was made to commemorate the foundation of American Cyborg as a company. Wilson had many conversations with us about the design and materials.

The object incorporates poplar, the Big and Little Dipper, rose quartz, lapis lazuli, bloodstone, graphite, gold, glass, and leather.
