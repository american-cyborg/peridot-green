Welcomet to Peridot Green

Peridot Green is the name we give to the space we live, work, and host. As a home, office, and gallery it has complex and interlocked needs.

For the seasonal salons, we have a rotating set of twelve themes. We regularly add new pieces to the permanent collection, and take on occasional loans and consignments.
